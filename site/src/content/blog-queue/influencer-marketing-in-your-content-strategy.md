<p>Finding influencers is the easy half of influencer marketing. The hard half is getting people spread across the country to film the right shots, the right way, on a budget and a deadline.</p>
<p>When Crocs launched a new shoe, it solved that second half with remote capture. Here is how it worked.</p>

<h2>Recruiting people who already love the brand</h2>
<p>Social media gives brands something they never had before: direct access to the people engaging with their products. The messaging tools on every major platform open a line straight to them.</p>
<p>That is not a license to spam DMs. It is a way to find real people who already interact with your brand and invite them to create with you. For more on that step, see <a href="/cinebody-blog/how-to-engage-micro-amp-nano-influencers-in-5-easy-steps">how to engage micro and nano influencers</a> and <a href="/cinebody-blog/the-top-5-platforms-to-find-nano-influencers">where to find nano influencers</a>.</p>
<p>But recruiting is only half the battle. Once your people are identified, you still need them to produce usable content.</p>

<h2>The launch: a polished ad plus a real-world view</h2>
<p>The occasion was a new Crocs shoe made from bio-based materials, designed to keep the look and feel of the clog people already loved.</p>
<p>Crocs used a traditional approach for the main spot: careful lighting, colorful backdrops, detailed animation, and handpicked talent, which made for a strong <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0X_5nokGMXw" target="_blank" rel="noopener">launch ad</a>. But Crocs has long balanced traditional production with user-generated content, because real footage builds a human connection a studio spot cannot.</p>
<p>That is where its network of digitally active influencers, each with an established following, came in.</p>

<h2>Shipping shoes and guiding the shots</h2>
<p>The moment Crocs wanted was simple: opening a box and seeing the new shoes for the first time. So the team shipped shoes to influencers across the country.</p>

<p>Using Cinebody and its shot list tools, influencers were guided through simple shots that documented the whole experience: the delivery, the unboxing, and everything in between. Nobody had to guess what to film.</p>

<h2>Consistent footage from many phones</h2>
<p>Different phones and different people usually mean mismatched footage. To avoid that, project managers set the filming specs on influencers' phones remotely, locking the resolution, frame rate, and orientation. The result was a cohesive set of clips that could stand next to traditionally shot content.</p>
<p>And it offered something the studio could not: the consumer's-eye view of receiving a new pair of Crocs.</p>

<h2>Watching footage land and steering the story</h2>
<p>Clips uploaded automatically, so the Crocs team could monitor footage as it arrived and give influencers direction. That made sure the key shots were captured leading up to the video's big finish.</p>
<p>The climax: influencers opened a box rigged with a spring-loaded mechanism that sent colorful confetti into the air. Surprise.</p>

<h2>What to borrow from the Crocs approach</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Use both kinds of video.</strong> The polished ad carried the product. The influencer footage carried the feeling.</li>
<li><strong>Design the moment.</strong> The confetti box gave every filmer the same big payoff to capture.</li>
<li><strong>Give a shot list, not a vibe.</strong> Delivery, unboxing, reaction. Clear steps produce clips an editor can cut. Our guide to <a href="/cinebody-blog/what-is-a-shot-list-and-how-do-i-write-one-for-a-ugc-campaign">writing a shot list for a UGC campaign</a> walks through it.</li>
<li><strong>Standardize the specs.</strong> Consistent orientation and quality keep a multi-person campaign looking like one piece.</li>
<li><strong>Review while people still have the box open.</strong> Direction mid-shoot beats a reshoot later.</li>
</ul>
<p>Today, Cinebody writes the shot list from a brief, coaches each filmer live on camera, and scores clips as they land. You can message filmers or send a direction that reaches their phone while the camera is up. Before you publish creator footage, make sure you have the <a href="/cinebody-blog/what-are-usage-rights">usage rights</a> sorted.</p>
