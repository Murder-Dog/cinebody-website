The problem this solves
You won't be on set. The app is.

With 13 offices and a fully remote talent brand team of one, most of what gets filmed for Kong will be shot by employees you're not standing next to. That's exactly what our newest platform release, rolling out in two stages this fall, was built for.

Phase 1 · shipping late September – October 2026
01

Brief becomes a shot list

Describe the concept (a day in the life, an office tour, a recruiting reel) and the app generates a full shot list in seconds, with orientation set per shot instead of per project.

02

Live AI director on set

Real-time checks for lighting, focus, framing, and audio while the employee is filming, with plain-language notes so they can fix it before they hit submit.

03

Clips scored automatically

Every upload gets scored on the same criteria, so you or your producer can sort straight to the strongest footage instead of watching every clip end to end.

04

Interview questions that adapt

An on-screen interviewer asks the scripted questions, then follows up in the moment based on the answer, useful for anyone who's never been on camera before.

Pricing
How the credits work

Credits scale with runtime, not headcount: a 30-second clip costs the same whether it's one person or ten.

6 credits
= one full video, 2–3 minutes
3 credits
= one short social cut, 15 sec – 1 min
Anytime
Top up credits as you go, larger purchases lower the per-credit rate

We'd recommend the credit package over a monthly retainer while you're still finding your cadence, since it's flexible for a year rather than committing to a fixed number of deliverables every month. Once you have a better read on volume, moving to a retainer or a fixed multi-month deliverable set is an easy switch.
