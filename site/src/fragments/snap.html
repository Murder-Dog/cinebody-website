The Challenge

Content at the speed of social.

Snap's goal was to make its own channels content-first. That took far more video than a traditional production model can deliver, and it had to feel like it came from real people, not a studio.

The target: 300 videos a month, posting 10 a day, across every major short-form platform.

Built with Contend. As Snap's agency, Contend set the creative direction and wrote the mini-scripts. Cinebody built the shot lists, managed the creators, and ran production.
