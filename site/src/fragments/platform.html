Projects & Idea Studio

From idea to plan in a minute

Stuck? Idea Studio deals you shoot ideas built on your brand; keep one and it becomes a project. Then generate a shot list automatically from the brief with plain directions, orientations, even slow-mo flags. Edit anything you'd like, invite your filmers, and you're live.

An idea becomes a plan · dealt, generated, editable
AI Director

Every filmer gets a director

The camera opens with the shot's brief right on screen, and Cinebody checks framing, light, focus, steadiness, and audio live while they film. When every check clears, they nailed it. First-take footage from anyone.

AI Director · web and the real capture screen
AI Voice Interviews

An interviewer in every pocket

For story shots, Cinebody interviews people out loud, listens to the answer, and asks the follow-up a director or producer would. Real stories pulled out of anyone, on any phone, in any city.

AI Voice Interview · run the shoot, it interviews on the phone
Messages, Directions & Map

Run the shoot from your desk

Watch clips land as people film. Message one filmer or all of them, or send a direction mid-shoot: it hits their phone as a notification while the camera is up. Course-correct in the moment, not after the footage is home.

Directions land as notifications, while they film
Library & AI Clip Review

Footage that grades itself

Every clip lands transcribed and scored: audio, stability, focus, exposure, framing, plus a plain verdict and what would make it better. Open any clip for the full review, and search everything by what people actually said.

AI Clip Review · web and app
Payouts

Incentivize your best filmers

The best footage deserves real money behind it: a flat rate or a per-clip bounty, your call per project. Filmers see what it pays before they film, connect Stripe once in the app, and the money lands the moment you approve.

Payouts · you fund it, filmers cash out in the app
Post Production

From a pile of clips to a timeline in one click

Cinebody reads all your footage, tells you the story it found, and one click builds the cut: best takes in order, B-roll over the seams, captions, music. Don't love it? Type a note like "get to the hook faster" and it revises just that.

Auto-Editor · the brief, then the cut
Brand Kit

Your brand, learned once

Point it at your website and it drafts your logo, colors, voice, and filming rules. From then on, every shot list, cover, caption, and cut stays on brand automatically.

Brand Kit · both pages, fetched from your site
Proof of Capture

Proof the footage is the footage

The moment a clip lands, Cinebody fingerprints the original file and signs a receipt: when it arrived, the phone it came from, and that nothing has changed since. The receipt stays attached through the edit, so the finished cut shows your client every clip inside it, and each one can be checked.
