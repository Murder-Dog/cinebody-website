<p>Most "blogs to follow" lists go stale fast. Sites stop posting, get folded into other brands, or drift into pure product news. Every source below was actively publishing when we checked in September 2026.</p>
<p>They are grouped by what you need: craft, workflow, gear, and the marketing side of video. Pick two or three that match your job instead of following all of them.</p>

<h2>Craft and storytelling</h2>
<h3><a href="https://nofilmschool.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">No Film School</a></h3>
<p>A broad, practical site for filmmakers at every level: screenwriting, directing, cinematography, editing, and industry news. <strong>Good for:</strong> learning story structure and technique in plain language, especially if you are new to production.</p>

<h3><a href="https://blog.frame.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Frame.io Insider</a></h3>
<p>Frame.io's publication for post-production people and creative teams. It mixes tactical how-to articles with product updates for the Frame.io review platform. <strong>Good for:</strong> understanding how professional review, approval, and delivery work, even if you never touch a timeline yourself.</p>

<h2>Post-production and workflow</h2>
<h3><a href="https://www.provideocoalition.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ProVideo Coalition</a></h3>
<p>A group of working editors, colorists, and writers covering post-production tools and workflows, often from hands-on use. <strong>Good for:</strong> honest takes on editing software, including how new AI assistants inside editing apps hold up on real jobs.</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.redsharknews.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">RedShark News</a></h3>
<p>News and analysis on moving-image technology, from cameras to broadcast and post. It reads more like a trade publication than a blog. <strong>Good for:</strong> keeping up with where production technology is heading, including trade show coverage like IBC.</p>

<h2>Cameras and gear</h2>
<h3><a href="https://www.cined.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CineD</a></h3>
<p>The site formerly known as cinema5D covers cameras, lenses, lighting, and audio for digital cinema. <strong>Good for:</strong> detailed gear news and reviews when you are specifying equipment for a crew or a studio.</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.videomaker.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Videomaker</a></h3>
<p>One of the longest-running video publications, founded as a magazine in 1986. It publishes reviews of cameras, microphones, lighting, and software along with production news. <strong>Good for:</strong> approachable reviews aimed at working shooters and small teams rather than big-budget sets.</p>

<h2>Video marketing</h2>
<h3><a href="https://wistia.com/blog" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Wistia</a></h3>
<p>The video hosting company's blog focuses on video for marketing, especially B2B. Its annual State of Video report draws on a survey of marketers and viewing data from its platform. <strong>Good for:</strong> planning where video goes and how to measure it, from webinars to LinkedIn.</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.socialmediatoday.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Social Media Today</a></h3>
<p>Daily coverage of new features, ad tools, and policy changes across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, and more. <strong>Good for:</strong> catching platform changes that affect how your video is shown, labeled, or advertised.</p>

<h3><a href="https://www.marketingbrew.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Marketing Brew</a></h3>
<p>A weekday newsletter on the marketing industry: brand strategy, creator marketing, sports marketing, and ad tech. <strong>Good for:</strong> a quick read on what other brand teams are doing, without leaving your inbox.</p>

<h3><a href="/cinebody-blog">The Cinebody blog</a></h3>
<p>Our own, so take this with a grain of salt. We write for brand and marketing teams who get video from real people on phones: <a href="/cinebody-blog/start-your-shot-list-from-the-brief">shot lists</a>, <a href="/cinebody-blog/5-steps-to-recording-interviews-iphone">phone interviews</a>, <a href="/cinebody-blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-capturing-b-roll">B-roll</a>, and <a href="/cinebody-blog/what-are-usage-rights">usage rights</a>. <strong>Good for:</strong> practical steps you can hand to employees, customers, or fans before they film.</p>

<h2>How to follow without the overload</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Match sources to decisions.</strong> Gear sites matter when you are buying. Marketing sources matter every week.</li>
<li><strong>Use a reader or a folder.</strong> When a site offers a newsletter or feed, send it to one place and skim once a week.</li>
<li><strong>Save, then apply.</strong> When a tip fits a real project, add it to your brief or shot list template so it actually changes the next shoot.</li>
</ul>
