<p>For about two years, the honest answer to "should we invest in TikTok?" was "maybe, if it survives." That question is mostly settled in the US. What is left is the practical one: does your audience spend time there, and can you make video that belongs in the feed?</p>

<h2>Where TikTok stands in the US</h2>
<p>A short timeline, because the headlines were confusing:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>January 17, 2025.</strong> The Supreme Court unanimously upheld the law requiring ByteDance to sell TikTok's US business or face a ban.</li>
<li><strong>January 18 to 19, 2025.</strong> The app went dark for US users on Saturday night and came back on Sunday after President-elect Trump said he would delay enforcement.</li>
<li><strong>January 22, 2026.</strong> TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC was established to run TikTok in the US, announced publicly the next day.</li>
</ul>
<p>Under the deal, ByteDance keeps 19.9%. Oracle, Silver Lake, and MGX each hold 15% as managing investors, with other investors holding the rest. Oracle is the security partner that hosts US data. The joint venture says it will retrain, test, and update the recommendation algorithm on US user data inside that secured environment.</p>
<p>For brands, the retraining is the part to watch. What worked in the feed before is not guaranteed to work the same way now. Treat the next stretch as a period to test, not to assume.</p>

<h2>Who is actually on it</h2>
<p>TikTok says the US joint venture serves more than 200 million Americans and 7.5 million businesses. Independent research gives a more conservative picture of adults. In Pew Research Center's 2025 survey, 37% of US adults said they use TikTok, up from 21% in 2021. Roughly half of 18- to 29-year-olds said they go on TikTok at least once a day, compared with 5% of adults 65 and older. Women were more likely to use it than men, 42% to 30%.</p>
<p>So the answer depends on who you sell to. If your buyers skew under 40, TikTok is hard to ignore. If they skew older or B2B, it may be a test channel rather than a core one.</p>

<h2>When TikTok is worth it, and when it is not</h2>
<p><strong>It tends to fit when:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Your product is visual, demonstrable, or has a community already talking about it.</li>
<li>You have people who can show up on camera: employees who know the work, customers who love the product, fans with opinions.</li>
<li>You can post consistently, not once a quarter.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>It tends to struggle when:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Every video needs weeks of legal and brand review.</li>
<li>The only content available is repurposed TV spots or polished horizontal ads.</li>
<li>Nobody owns the channel week to week.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What brand video that fits TikTok looks like</h2>
<p>TikTok's own creative guidance for advertisers comes back to the same points: shoot vertical, get to the point immediately, design for sound, use on-screen text, and make it feel native to the platform rather than like an ad dropped into the feed. That describes video real people make on their phones.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Vertical from the start.</strong> Frame for 9:16 when you shoot. Cropping a horizontal clip later loses faces and products. Our guide to <a href="/cinebody-blog/how-to-frame-video-on-smartphones">framing video on smartphones</a> covers this.</li>
<li><strong>Hook in the first seconds.</strong> Open on the result, the reaction, or the problem. Save the logo for later.</li>
<li><strong>Clear sound.</strong> A person speaking should be heard over the room. Get close, pick a quiet spot, and check a take before you move on.</li>
<li><strong>Real people, real places.</strong> An employee on the floor or a customer at home reads as more believable than a set.</li>
<li><strong>Text on screen.</strong> TikTok's guidance recommends text overlays, and a caption helps the point land even when sound is off.</li>
<li><strong>More takes, shorter clips.</strong> TikTok has raised its length limits far past its original 15 seconds, but short clips still give an editor options for a tight cut.</li>
</ul>
<p>Lo-fi does not mean careless. Good light, a steady phone, and audible audio are what separate authentic from unwatchable. Our post on <a href="/cinebody-blog/lo-fi-video-content">lo-fi video content</a> goes deeper.</p>
