AI data handling

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

Overview

Cinebody's AI features help people film, review and edit. They send footage, frames or audio to a short list of vendors under business API terms. Under those terms, the AI vendors (Anthropic, Google and ElevenLabs) don't train their models on it. Cinebody keeps footage only while the project and account exist, and deletes it on request at any level, down to one clip.

  • Filming: the Director and AI interview guide people in real time while they record.
  • Review: clips are transcribed and scored on production quality, such as framing, light, audio and stability.
  • Editing: captions, audio clean-up and finished cuts are made in Cinebody's editor.

Each vendor gets only what its job needs. Full footage stays with Cinebody's storage vendors; the AI vendors get frames, clips or audio for one task.

What Cinebody keeps, and for how long

Cinebody keeps a workspace's footage and the AI results made from it until someone deletes them. Nothing expires on its own while the workspace is active.

Data Where it lives Kept until
Clips (video and audio) Mux and Cloudflare R2 Deleted, then purged from Trash after 30 days
Transcripts and clip reviews (scores, notes) Cinebody's database The clip is deleted
Shot lists, Director notes, messages Cinebody's database The project is deleted
Finished edits and renders Cloudflare R2 The project or workspace is deleted
Payout records for paid filmers Cinebody's database, anonymized Four years, for tax records

All of a workspace's data is separate from every other workspace's. One client's footage never appears in another client's projects. Training is covered below.

Deleting data

Any piece of data can be deleted, from one clip up to a whole workspace. A deletion removes the files from storage and the transcripts and AI reviews made from them.

What How When it's gone
One clip Delete it in the project Purged from Trash after 30 days
One project Delete the project Purged from Trash after 30 days, with all its clips, transcripts, reviews and edits
One person (filmer or interviewee) Email support@cinebody.com Within 30 days of verifying the request
A whole workspace Email support@cinebody.com Within 30 days of verifying the request

Deleting a person removes their profile, every clip they filmed, and the transcripts and reviews of those clips, across all projects. A finished video that was already delivered stays, because it's delivered work. Deleting the client's workspace removes it.

Vendors use what they receive only to do their task, and keep it only as long as the vendor table and their DPAs allow.

Training

Each workspace has its own training setting, and only a workspace admin can change it (Settings → Team → Training). It's on by default, and we can switch it off for a client before they start. When it's off, nothing from that workspace is used to train or improve Cinebody's AI.

  • Off takes nothing away. Every feature works the same with training off.
  • It applies from the moment it's switched off. Use before that point isn't undone.
  • Every change is logged, with who changed it and when, in the workspace's audit log.
  • Vendor training is separate. The setting covers Cinebody's own use. Anthropic's, Google's and ElevenLabs' terms already rule out training on our data.

What the visual AI looks at

The AI judges the shot, not the person. It checks what makes footage usable and nothing about who someone is.

Looks at Never looks at or stores
Whether a face is in frame, head angle, distance from camera Identity, facial geometry or face prints
Framing, motion, stability, lighting Age, race, gender or other sensitive traits
Audio level and clarity Voice prints
Common objects in the shot Profiles that follow a person across projects

Each interview session stands alone. The AI interviewer doesn't remember a person from one session to the next.
