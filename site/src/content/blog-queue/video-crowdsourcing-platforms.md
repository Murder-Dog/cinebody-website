<p>"Crowdsourced video" covers very different businesses. Some platforms run creative competitions, some hand you a curated filmmaker, some are marketplaces for hiring freelancers, and some help your own people film. Picking the wrong model is how a brand ends up paying for polish when it needed volume, or getting volume when it needed a story.</p>
<p>We first published this list years ago. Since then, some of the platforms on it have gone quiet and others have changed what they do. Here is the current version, checked against each company's own site, with a plain note on who each one suits.</p>

<h2>Competition and curated filmmaker models</h2>
<h3>1. Tongal</h3>
<p><a href="https://tongal.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Tongal</a> connects brands and studios with a global community of writers, directors, and filmmakers. Its classic structure runs in phases: a brand posts a project with prize money, the community submits short ideas, creators pitch how they would execute the winners, and selected pitches get funded to produce. Tongal also builds longer-running programs. With the LEGO Group it created LEGO World Builder, where a fan's pitch was picked to become an episode of the Netflix series LEGO DREAMZzz.</p>
<p><strong>Good fit:</strong> brands that want a wide spread of concepts and are comfortable judging work in stages.</p>

<h3>2. MOFILM</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.mofilm.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MOFILM</a> is part of The Brandtech Group, which describes it as a global community of more than 10,000 filmmakers. The model has moved toward curation. MOFILM says it no longer posts live briefs for creators to bid on. Instead, it develops the brief with the client, identifies creators from its community based on skills, location, and experience, and lets the client choose.</p>
<p><strong>Good fit:</strong> brands that want a produced piece from a matched filmmaker, with a team managing the project.</p>

<h2>Marketplace and network models</h2>
<h3>3. Glimmer</h3>
<p><a href="https://glimmer.io/home/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Glimmer</a>, formerly Storyhunter, is a marketplace and management platform for hiring freelance video producers. It lists more than 50,000 creators available for hire, tools for building and paying your own creator network, and Glimmer Select, a managed production option for larger jobs.</p>
<p><strong>Good fit:</strong> teams that need a professional videographer or crew in a city where they have no contacts.</p>

<h3>4. Vidmob</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.vidmob.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Vidmob</a> is best known today for creative data: scoring and analyzing ads against platform and brand best practices. In June 2026 it launched Vidmob360, which brings that creative data into the AI tools and media workflows brands already use. Vidmob also offers Creative Studio, backed by a curated network of freelancers vetted and badged by skill, for producing and adapting ad creative.</p>
<p><strong>Good fit:</strong> performance teams that want production tied closely to ad measurement.</p>

<h2>Community capture models</h2>
<h3>5. Cinebody</h3>
<p>This is our platform, so weigh this entry accordingly. <a href="/platform">Cinebody</a> is not a marketplace. Brands use it to get video from people who already know them: employees, customers, fans, and athletes. You send a link, and people film on their phones with no app download. Cinebody writes the shot list from a plain-English brief, coaches each filmer live on camera, scores every clip as it lands, and cuts a finished video. Brands can pay filmers with flat rates or per-clip bounties if they choose.</p>
<p><strong>Good fit:</strong> brands that need a steady flow of real, vertical video from their own community. Not built to replace a large hero production.</p>

<h2>How to choose between them</h2>
<p>Start with what you need back, not with the platform.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>One big creative idea.</strong> A competition model surfaces many concepts before you commit budget.</li>
<li><strong>One polished piece.</strong> A curated filmmaker or managed production gets you a single strong edit.</li>
<li><strong>A crew somewhere far away.</strong> A freelance marketplace is the direct route.</li>
<li><strong>Lots of believable video, often.</strong> Your own community, coached well, is hard to beat for volume and credibility.</li>
</ul>
<p>Whichever route you take, the brief decides the outcome. Our guide to <a href="/cinebody-blog/crowdsourcing-a-video-campaign-read-these-5-tips-first">planning a crowdsourced video campaign</a> covers the setup, and <a href="/cinebody-blog/start-your-shot-list-from-the-brief">starting your shot list from the brief</a> shows how to turn a goal into shots people can actually film. Check <a href="/cinebody-blog/what-are-usage-rights">usage rights</a> on any platform before you publish.</p>
