Sub-processors

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Super 6 LLC d/b/a Cinebody ("Cinebody") uses the third-party service providers below to deliver the Cinebody platform. Each one may process personal data that our customers and their teams upload or create in Cinebody, only to provide its service to Cinebody and under written data protection terms.

None of these providers may use Cinebody customer data to train or improve their own AI models. Where a provider offers a setting that allows training on customer data, we keep it turned off.

This list covers providers that process customer personal data. It does not include tools that never receive it. How we handle personal data is described in our Privacy Policy.
