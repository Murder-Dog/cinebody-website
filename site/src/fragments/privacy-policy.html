Automatic Collection of Personal Information

Cinebody, our service providers, and our advertising partners may automatically log or collect information about individuals or their devices, and their activity over time on our Services and other sites and online services, such as:

  • Browsing Information, such as browsing history, search history, information about the pages, features, projects, and content you access or use through the Services, referral source information, and other online activity data.
  • Mobile App Usage Information, such as information about your mobile device and use of the application, including a device identifier, operating system, version and other information about your mobile device as well as time spent in different portions of the Cinebody mobile application, which we may automatically receive when you download or use our mobile app.
  • Device and Technical Information, such as IP address, technical session information, browser type, information about your computer, hardware, software, or mobile device, including a device identifier, device battery and storage information navigational data including log files, server logs, clickstream data, and other data such as your location, where enabled, and language preference.

The Services may use different types of tracking technologies to collect these categories along with other analytics, attribution, advertising, and campaign-performance information. Those technologies may include, for example:

  • Cookies, which are text files that websites store on a visitor's device for purposes such as remembering preferences, recording session information (such as pages visited, interactions with website features, time spent on the website, etc.), aiding navigation, enabling functionality, and supporting analytics and advertising.
  • Flash Cookies, also known as “local shared objects,” which operate differently than browser cookies and cannot be removed or blocked via web browser settings.
  • Web Beacons, also known as pixels, tags, or clear GIFs, which are tiny graphics with a unique identifier, similar in function to cookies, and are used to track the online movements of users, such as demonstrating that a webpage or email was accessed or opened, or that certain content was viewed or clicked. In contrast to cookies, which are stored on a user's device or browser, clear gifs are embedded invisibly on websites or in email messages. Web beacons are typically used to compile statistics about usage of websites and the success of marketing campaigns.
  • Local Storage, which is used to save data on a visitor's device. We may use data from local storage for similar reasons as cookies, such as to store multimedia preferences, customize what we show you based on your past interactions with our Platform, remember your preferences, and measure ad effectiveness.
  • Software Development Kits, or SDKs, which are types of computer code embedded in mobile apps for a variety of purposes, such as collecting analytics, adding features or functionality, or facilitating online advertising. SDKs may also enable third parties to collect information directly via our mobile app. For example, we monitor software usage to help us develop the design and layout of the mobile application and to improve the content to better match the interests of our users. We may also collect different types of information about your location, including general information (e.g., IP address, zip code) and more specific information (e.g., GPS-based functionality on mobile devices used to access the Platform), and may use that information to customize the Services. If you access the Services through a mobile device and you do not want your device to provide us with location-tracking information, you can disable the GPS or other location-tracking functions on your device. See your device manufacturer's instructions for further details.
  • Chat technologies, to operate any chat features that you may use to communicate with us through the Platform. Our chat vendors and other third parties may access and use the device and online activity information described above and monitor and record your chats for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. In most cases, when you use these features, you are communicating with a digital agent and not a human.
